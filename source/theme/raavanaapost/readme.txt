News theme for RaavanaaPost. Community needs. Real impact.

== Description ==

Green, gold and orange from the RaavanaaPost logo, with the crown, the laurels
and the king carried through every page.

* Front page: top story, latest stories, Most read, the gold
  "Community needs. Real impact." band, Sri Lanka, Canada and the diaspora,
  Culture, Sports, Voices, Report a need and the Morning brief sign-up.
* Need tracker: mark any story as a community need and give it a stage
  (Initiated, Activated, Impact). It shows on the front page, on the story,
  and on the "Needs we are following" page.
* Report a need form and Morning brief sign-up. Everything sent is kept
  privately in the dashboard under RaavanaaPost, and new reports are emailed
  to the site's admin email.
* One-click setup under RaavanaaPost > Start here: sections, menus, pages and
  sample stories. Sample stories can be removed in one click.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress dashboard go to Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme >
   Upload Theme.
2. Choose raavanaapost.zip, then Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open RaavanaaPost > Start here and press "Set up my site".

== Writing a story ==

* Posts > Add New. Add the headline, the story and a featured image.
* The Excerpt is the short summary under the headline.
* In the "Story details" box, add the place (for example Jaffna). If the story
  is a community need, choose its stage and add a one-line update.
* Tick "Stick to the top of the blog" to make a story the big lead.

== Changing words and links ==

Appearance > Customize > RaavanaaPost words and links: the green band, the
footer line, social links and a Tamil edition link. A full Tamil edition can be
added later with the free Polylang plugin; the theme shows its language switch
at the top automatically.
