Why we do this

Some children grow up without ever going to a game, seeing a new place or sitting down to a really good meal. Nobody close to them can afford it, and childhood does not wait.

At the same time, all over the world there are people who would gladly give a child one of those days, if only they knew where it was needed and could trust that it would truly reach the child.

Raavanaa brings the two together.

What a project is

A game

Bowling, an arcade, a day of play.

A place

A trip to somewhere they have never seen.

A good meal

A delicious dish, shared together, once in a while.

Our promise

  • Every need is checked in person before a project is shared here.
  • Every penny goes to the project itself. Nothing is kept for running costs.
  • Every project shows its budget and who made it possible.
  • Everyone who gave sees what their kindness did, with a story and pictures after the day.
Children come first
  • We share photos and first names only with permission from a parent, a guardian or the home that cares for the child.
  • We never post a child's full name, address or school.
  • On our map we mark the town, never a home or a school.

Be part of it

"Beyond the Lanes" is our promise to step out of our own lanes and into the lives of the children around us.
