Mums and kids, united: a day out together in Kilinochchi

Sack races, a tug of war the mums won, and a long lunch in the shade.

RPby RaavanaaPost team · 1 November 2026 · Kilinochchi Visited and verified

Project at a glance

Status
Complete
Location
Kilinochchi
Started
August 2026
Reach
18 mums and 30 children
18mums
30children
1day together

For many of these mums, days off are rare, and days out with their children rarer still. So the day was theirs: no cooking and no chores, only time together.

A bus took 18 mums and 30 children out for the day. They played sack races and a tug of war, which the mums won, then sat down to a long lunch in the shade. For many mums it was the first day in years with no work to do. Every family went home with a printed photo of the day.

Who made it possible

Sasi, Shanker and Bala gave $150 each, $450 in all: the bus ($150), lunch for mums and children ($180), games and activities ($70) and a printed family photo for each mum ($50).
