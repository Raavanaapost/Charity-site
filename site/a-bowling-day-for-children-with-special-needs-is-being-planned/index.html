A day out for a whole group of children with special needs takes transport, extra helpers and a budget, so it does not happen often. These are exactly the days a child remembers.

The plan

A full day at the bowling lanes, where every child gets a turn and every roll gets a cheer, with transport, food and helpers arranged for the group.

The budget for the day has been requested. As soon as it is ready, we will share it here, and the project can move to Activated.
