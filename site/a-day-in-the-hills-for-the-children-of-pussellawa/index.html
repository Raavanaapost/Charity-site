“The children truly enjoyed the experience and were very happy.”

Pussellawa is a tea-country town, and many of its children grow up among the estates. Families work hard, and a day away together is rare. Transport and food are what stand between these children and a day out.

On 12 October 2026 a hired vehicle took the children to Hanuman Kovil, in the hills on the road between Gampola and Nuwara Eliya. On the big lawn they played running games, held hands in circles and danced, while the grown-ups watched over them. Some of the little ones simply stood and looked out at the mountains.

The children truly enjoyed the experience and were very happy.

The organisers in Pussellawa, on the day

Where the money went

Every rupee went to the day itself: Rs 10,500 for the vehicle and Rs 8,750 for food, Rs 19,250 in total, covered by Friends of Raavanaa. It was the first Raavanaa day in the hill country.
