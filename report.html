Your voice keeps us honest

Report a concern

If anything about a visit, a project day, a team member or the money did not feel right, tell us. Small or serious, named or anonymous.

Who reads itOur safeguarding lead and one independent reviewer from outside the project team. The person you write about never sees your report.
What happens nextEvery report is logged, looked into and closed with a written outcome. If you leave a way to reach you, we reply within 2 days.
Never a problem for youReporting will never affect help for your children, your home or your community.

A child in danger right now? Do not wait for us. In Sri Lanka call the National Child Protection Authority on 1929 or the police on 119.
