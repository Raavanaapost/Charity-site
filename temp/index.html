How it works

Four steps, and you know the fee before the second one.

  1. A short call

    You tell me about your year. I tell you what I need and what it will cost.

  2. Send your documents

    Drop them off or send copies. I'll flag anything missing so we only do this once.

  3. Review it together

    We go through the return line by line. Nothing is filed until you've seen it and agreed.

  4. Filed and confirmed

    I file electronically and send you the confirmation, plus a copy of everything for your records.
