    Common questions

    What do I need to bring?

    It depends on your year. The checklist on this page lists the usual documents for employees, the self-employed, landlords, students and retirees.

    When is my return due?

    For most people, April 30. If you or your spouse are self-employed you have until June 15 to file, but any balance you owe is still due April 30. For the 2026 return, RRSP contributions count if you make them by March 1, 2027.

    I'm behind on my taxes. Can you help?

    Yes. We work out which years and slips are missing, file the returns in order and get you back to current.

    I got a letter from the CRA. What should I do?

    Don't ignore it, and note the reply date on the letter. Send me a copy and I'll tell you what they're asking for and what to send back.

    Do I have to come to the office?

    No. You can drop documents off at the Scarborough office or send copies, and we can go through your return by phone.
